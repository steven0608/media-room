Turn on your microphone

Karaoke uses it so you hear yourself through the speakers and get a score. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

  1. Press the Meta button and open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Still Water → Permissions (or Privacy & safety → Microphone).
  3. Switch Microphone on, come back, and press Try again.
